A three-line formula for any type: name the feeling ("I got tense just now"), own it or set a boundary ("That was my mistake / this bothers me"), propose a next step together ("Next time I'll try this. You?").

다른 버전

실수구버전 "억울해, 어쩔 수 없었어" → 새 버전 "내 실수야. 다음엔 이렇게"
취향구버전 "아무거나" → 새 버전 "난 A가 좋아. B도 열려 있어"

전자는 "아직 자라는 중", 후자는 "같이 미래를 그릴 사람"으로 읽힙니다.

Same moment, different versions. Mistake: "It wasn't my fault" vs "My mistake, next time I'll do this." Asked your opinion: "Whatever" vs "I'd like A, but I'm open to B." One reads as still growing, one as a partner.
