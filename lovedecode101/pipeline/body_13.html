Distance isn't disowning. Cutting off ends the bond and leaves resentment. Setting distance draws a line around your space and keeps the bond. The goal isn't changing them, it's turning down their volume inside you.

설득하지 마세요. 이해받으려 할수록 다시 평가의 자리로 돌아갑니다. 선은 설명이 아니라 반복으로 지켜집니다.

Boundaries, short and calm: "I'll decide that one." "Thanks for worrying, but I'm okay." "Let's stop here for today." Don't try to convince. Seeking approval puts you back on trial. Boundaries hold by repetition.

The most practical step: one minute every morning. Breathe in 4, out 6. Say aloud, hand on chest: "I am not my parents' portrait." "I live by my own choices." "Today, my way." Your first sentence weakens theirs.
