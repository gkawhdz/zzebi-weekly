Look at balance, not background. Wealth or a pampered upbringing tells you nothing. The give-and-take rhythm right now tells you everything. If only you are draining money, time and heart, that's the signal.

가장 정직한 측정기는 내 몸입니다. 머리는 "좋은 사람이야"라고 해도 몸은 알고 있습니다.

  • 충전돼서 돌아온다 → 균형
  • 좋았는데 이상하게 녹초 → 기울어짐

일주일만 기록해보세요. 패턴이 보입니다.

The most practical test: check your battery on the way home. Your body is the honest meter. Recharged means balance. "It was nice, but I'm drained" means it's tilting. Track it for a week.

If this sounds like you: noticing is where change starts. Say one thing you received each day out loud. Turn "do this for me" into "here's how I felt." When you crave reassurance, wait ten minutes first.
